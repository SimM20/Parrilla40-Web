Privacy Policy

Draft dated October 29, 2026. Not yet effective: the privacy email, provider and transfer verification, and operational retention procedure are pending.

Controller and contact

Reported controller: Parrilla40.SA. Address: Av gelly y obes 2267, Recoleta, CABA, 1425, Argentina.

The privacy contact email has not been provided yet. Do not post sensitive information in survey text. This draft does not replace an operational contact channel.

Data and purposes

  • Playtest: two selections about your experience and, if you wish, a written memory of up to 1200 characters. Also a random identifier, submission date and game build. These support experience research and build comparisons.
  • Optional Steam notice: email, language, consent date and version, and a fingerprint of the withdrawal code. These are stored separately, without an identifier or link to your answers.
  • Existing event leaderboard: publicly displays a name or alias, best earnings and number of runs received from the game, in a separate database. This survey does not collect those data or link them to your answers.
  • Site operation: theme preference and temporary submission status. While confirmation is pending, the tab session retains survey answers or, separately, the email and code for a pending signup. These temporary data are removed when each operation is confirmed. See cookies and storage.

The survey does not request a name, account, phone, email or demographic profile. Q1 and Q2 are needed to submit; Q3 is optional. Responses are not linked to an identity, but text may identify you if you include personal information: please avoid doing so. The form does not store an IP address with answers; providers may process IP addresses and technical logs needed to deliver and protect the service.

Participation and consent

Participation is voluntary and you can leave before submitting. No email is required to complete the survey. Notice registration has its own form and an initially unchecked box: it authorizes only one notice when Parrilla 40 can be added to a Steam wishlist. It does not authorize other promotions or the sale of your data.

There is no profiling or automated decision-making about people.

Access, providers and transfers

Authorized team members access research results. Supabase provides the database service; jsDelivr delivers the connection library on pages that use it. The hosting provider serves the site. Response and email lists are not public. Valid legal requests may be fulfilled.

The survey and email database is hosted by Supabase in us-west-2 (Oregon, United States). Subprocessors, the hosting provider and other processing destinations still need confirmation. Before the final version is published, destinations and an applicable mechanism under Argentine rules will be identified. A contract from another jurisdiction is not assumed to meet all these requirements. See AAIP information on transfers and the Supabase DPA.

Retention and deletion

Proposed policy, pending operational implementation: review data monthly and remove individual responses 12 months after submission; retain only summaries that cannot identify people afterward. Previous questionnaire data remains separate during this migration and its continued necessity will be reviewed.

New email signups will be deleted upon consent withdrawal, 30 days after the single notice, or 12 months after registration, whichever happens first. Specific information may be kept if legally required, for the applicable period. Provider log and backup retention periods still need confirmation; immediate deletion from their backups is not promised.

The notice given to event participants, their permission to publish the leaderboard, and that list's retention period must be checked with the event organizer.

Your rights and withdrawing the notice

You may request access to, correction, updating or deletion of your personal data. Under AAIP guidance, access requests are answered within 10 calendar days; correction, updating or deletion within 5 business days. Free access may be requested every six months, or sooner when justified by a legitimate interest.

For the notice, save the secret withdrawal link or code shown when you register. An existing signup keeps its original link; a new one does not replace it. Use Withdraw consent to delete the signup without answering the survey again. Anyone holding that code can cancel the signup: keep it private. If you lose it, ownership of the email needs proportionate verification through the controller's contact channel, which is still pending.

A response cannot be located from an email because they are stored separately. If you need to ask about text containing your personal information, the controller must seek a proportionate way to locate it without requesting unnecessary data. You may complain to the Argentine Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP), which oversees Law 25,326, or pursue the appropriate judicial remedy.

Security and changes

The implementation restricts public access and separates research from notices; internal access requires server validation. Configuration and permissions must be checked in the actual deployment. No system removes all risk. Changes to purposes or providers will be reflected here, with new consent requested when required.

Initial legal framework: Argentine Law 25,326 and Decree 1558/2001. The Spanish and English versions describe the same processing and do not reduce applicable legal rights.
